# 64th Annual Congress of the Indian Society of Gastroenterology (ISGCON 2023)

## Identity
- **Organization:** Indian Society of Gastroenterology — ISGCON 2023 Organising Committee, Bengaluru
- **Event:** 64th Annual Congress of the Indian Society of Gastroenterology (ISGCON 2023)
- **Year:** 2023
- **Location:** Bengaluru — venue needs confirmation (see ambiguity note)
- **Identity confidence:** HIGH for the event — 64th ISGCON, 21–24 December 2023, Bengaluru, 2,000+ delegates. MEDIUM for the venue.
- **Ambiguity note:** Aggregators (eMedEvents, 10times) name 'Dr Babu Rajendra Prasad International Convention Centre'. The archived official site instead describes a venue 'in the northern part of Bengaluru, on Bengaluru-Hyderabad highway at about 14 km'. These do not obviously agree. Confirm with the organising committee before citing a venue. Also note a distinct '64th ISGCON 2024' article exists — do not conflate; the source record is the 2023 Bengaluru edition.
