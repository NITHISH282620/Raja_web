# Hampi Utsav 2024 (Hampi Festival / Vijaya Utsav)

## Identity
- **Organization:** Karnataka State Habitat Centre (client of record). The festival itself is staged by the Government of Karnataka with the Vijayanagara district administration.
- **Event:** Hampi Utsav 2024 (Hampi Festival / Vijaya Utsav)
- **Year:** 2024
- **Location:** Hampi, Vijayanagara district, Karnataka — UNESCO World Heritage Site
- **Identity confidence:** EVENT: HIGH. Three-day state festival at Hampi in early February 2024, staged by the Government of Karnataka against the UNESCO-listed ruins, with parades, elephants, cultural stages, fireworks and a light-and-sound show. CLIENT ROLE: MEDIUM — the Karnataka State Habitat Centre is named as Raja's client but is not the festival's public organiser; it is presumably the implementing or procuring agency.
- **Ambiguity note:** TWO CAUTIONS. (1) DATE CONFLICT: sources give both 2–4 February and 3–5 February 2024. Confirm before captioning. (2) YEAR TRAP: the Deccan Herald article headlined 'Hampi Utsav: Construction of main stage begins' — which is the single most relevant press item for Raja's kind of work — is served from an image path dated 2025-02-12, so it very probably covers the 2025 edition, NOT 2024. Do not cite it as 2024 evidence without checking the byline date. Also note Hampi Utsav has been cancelled in some years, so never assume an annual run.
