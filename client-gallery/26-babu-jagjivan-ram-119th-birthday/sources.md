# 119th birth anniversary of Dr Babu Jagjivan Ram

## Identity
- **Organization:** Karnataka State Marketing Communication and Advertisement Ltd (KSMCAL), Government of Karnataka
- **Event:** 119th birth anniversary of Dr Babu Jagjivan Ram
- **Year:** 2026 (most likely)
- **Location:** Bengaluru — presumed; venue not established
- **Identity confidence:** OCCASION: HIGH — Babu Jagjivan Ram was born on 5 April 1908, and his Jayanti is observed on 5 April each year. He was a freedom fighter, long-serving union minister and Deputy Prime Minister, and a leading advocate for the scheduled castes. In Karnataka the anniversary is observed by district administrations together with Zilla Panchayats, the Department of Social Welfare and the Dr B R Ambedkar Development Corporation. YEAR: MEDIUM — see the counting note below. VENUE: UNRESOLVED.
- **Ambiguity note:** ORDINAL/YEAR AMBIGUITY. Counting from a birth year of 1908, the 118th anniversary falls in 2026 and the 119th in 2027; Indian official usage frequently advances the ordinal by one, which would make the '119th' 5 April 2026. Given the source record exists now, 5 April 2026 is the most likely date — but confirm against Raja's work order rather than relying on the arithmetic. Note also that documented Karnataka observances of this anniversary have been at DISTRICT level (Chikkamagaluru's Kuvempu Kalamandira for the 110th, Madikeri's Old Fort Hall for the 112th), so a Bengaluru location should not be assumed.
