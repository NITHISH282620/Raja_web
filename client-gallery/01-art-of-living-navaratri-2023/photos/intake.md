# Supplied photography — intake assessment

Source: `THE ART OF LIVING TRUST` (client-supplied batch, 26 Aug 2026)

Selected files are in `photos/`. Anything rejected is listed below and was not copied; 
anything belonging to a different event or year is quarantined in `_mismatched/`.


## Caveat

**These are web-sourced images of Art of Living mass events, not date-verified to Navaratri 2023.** The Art of Living stages several events at this scale each year (Navratri, Mahashivratri, anniversary celebrations), and none of the three carries a visible date. They are visually excellent and on-brand for the client, but must not be captioned 'Navaratri 2023' until Raja or the Trust confirms the edition. Two further supplied files were explicitly labelled Mahashivratri 2024 and were rejected as a different festival.
