# Krishimela 2024 — UAS Bangalore agricultural fair

## Identity
- **Organization:** University of Agricultural Sciences, Bangalore (The Comptroller, UAS)
- **Event:** Krishimela 2024 — UAS Bangalore agricultural fair
- **Year:** 2024
- **Location:** Gandhi Krishi Vignana Kendra (GKVK) campus, Bengaluru
- **Identity confidence:** HIGH — 14–17 November 2024 at GKVK, Bengaluru, themed 'Climate Smart Digital Agriculture'. Around 700 stalls reported at opening, with UAS itself citing ~740 agricultural exhibition stalls erected. Opening-day footfall reported at 1.31 lakh; Deccan Herald reported the fair concluding with 54.16 lakh visitors.
- **Ambiguity note:** The source record reads 'KRISHIMELA 2024-25', which spans a financial year rather than an edition. Krishimela is annual, so confirm whether Raja worked the November 2024 edition, the 2025 edition, or both. Do NOT confuse with the unrelated 'Krishi Mela and Exhibition' run by Sasya Shyamala Krishi Vigyan Kendra (7–9 February 2024, West Bengal). Note GKVK is the SAME CAMPUS as record #05 (EIMA Agrimach 2024) — two large agricultural exhibitions on one site.
