# World Fisheries Day 2024 — Matsya Mela (Fish Fair), Bengaluru

## Identity
- **Organization:** Skyblue Event Management India Private Limited (client of record). The event is staged by the Karnataka Department of Fisheries.
- **Event:** World Fisheries Day 2024 — Matsya Mela (Fish Fair), Bengaluru
- **Year:** 2024
- **Location:** Bengaluru — exact venue not established
- **Identity confidence:** MEDIUM-HIGH. World Fisheries Day falls on 21 November; the 2024 national theme was 'India's Blue Transformation: Strengthening Small-Scale and Sustainable Fisheries'. Karnataka's Department of Fisheries marks it with a three-day Matsya Mela (Fish Fair) in Bengaluru featuring roughly 52 stalls, exhibits, discussions and food stalls. The Bengaluru venue for the 2024 edition has not been confirmed.
- **Ambiguity note:** YEAR TRAP — the ETV Bharat 'Bengaluru Fish Fair opens to public' article carries the article id enn25112105768, which decodes to 21 November 2025, i.e. the 2025 Matsya Mela, not 2024. Its 52-stall and timing details may therefore describe the wrong edition. Separately, do not confuse the Karnataka state Matsya Mela with the NATIONAL World Fisheries Day ceremony, which was held at Sushma Swaraj Bhawan in New Delhi and inaugurated by the Union Minister. Raja's client is an event management company, so Raja is likely a sub-vendor to Skyblue rather than contracted by the department.
